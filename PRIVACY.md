# Peek-a-Tab Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

Peek-a-Tab does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

**What the extension reads.** To show you your open windows, Peek-a-Tab reads the titles, addresses
(URLs) and site icons of your open tabs, using the browser's `tabs` permission. This happens
locally, on your device, and only to display the list in the extension's popup.

**What it remembers.** The `storage` permission is used to keep a short list of window numbers, in the
order you last used them, so the most recently used window can be shown first. This list lives in
the browser's temporary session storage and is cleared when you close the browser. It contains no
tab titles, URLs or other content.

**What it sends.** Nothing. Peek-a-Tab makes no network requests, has no servers, and includes no
analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

**Contact.** Questions or concerns: please open an issue at https://github.com/BluDotz/peek-a-tab/issues
